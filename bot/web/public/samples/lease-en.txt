RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Tashkent                                                  1 October 2026

This Lease Agreement is made between Rustam Karimov ("Landlord") and Anna Petrova ("Tenant").

1. PROPERTY AND TERM
1.1. The Landlord leases to the Tenant the apartment at 12 Amir Temur Street, Apt. 37, Tashkent.
1.2. The lease term is 12 months from the date of signing.

2. RENT AND DEPOSIT
2.1. Monthly rent is USD 450, payable in advance by the 1st day of each month.
2.2. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time by giving the Tenant 3 days' notice.
2.3. The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of USD 900. The deposit is non-refundable.
2.4. For each day of late payment, the Tenant shall pay a penalty of 5% of the monthly rent.

3. USE OF THE PROPERTY
3.1. The Landlord may enter the apartment at any time without prior notice to the Tenant.
3.2. The Tenant is responsible for all repairs of any kind, including structural repairs, at the Tenant's own expense.
3.3. The Tenant may not have guests stay overnight.

4. TERMINATION
4.1. The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving 1 day's notice.
4.2. If the Tenant terminates this Agreement early, the Tenant shall pay the full rent for the entire remaining term.
4.3. This Agreement renews automatically for another 12 months unless the Tenant gives written notice at least 90 days before the end of the term.

5. OTHER
5.1. Any dispute shall be resolved exclusively by the court at the Landlord's place of residence.
5.2. This Agreement is made in two copies.
